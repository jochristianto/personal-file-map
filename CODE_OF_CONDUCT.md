# Code of Conduct

Bills Overview is a small project run in people's spare time. We want everyone who reports a bug, asks a question or sends a pull request to feel welcome, whatever their experience, background or identity.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers and with people writing in a second language.
- Assume good intent. Ask before concluding someone meant harm.
- Critique ideas and code, not people. Explain *why* when you disagree.
- Accept that maintainers have the final say on what gets merged, and that "no" or "not now" is a valid answer.
- Respect privacy. Bills are personal: never post someone else's files, screenshots or details.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, slurs, or demeaning comments about anyone's identity or abilities.
- Sexual language or imagery, or unwanted attention.
- Threats, or encouraging anyone to harm themselves or others.
- Publishing someone's private information (addresses, account numbers, bills, emails) without their permission.
- Repeatedly pushing a topic after being asked to stop, or deliberately derailing discussions.

## Where it applies

In the repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, commits) and anywhere you represent the project in public.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour bothers you, contact the maintainers privately through the email address on the maintainer's GitHub profile, or by opening a [private security advisory](../../security/advisories/new) if you need a private channel inside GitHub. Include links or screenshots. Reports are kept confidential.

## What happens

Maintainers review every report and decide what's fair, from a private word, to a public warning, to removing comments, to a temporary or permanent ban from the project. We'll tell the person who reported back what we did, as far as privacy allows.

This code of conduct is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
